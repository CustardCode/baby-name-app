Privacy at Baby Names Australia

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Information collected by this website

Current website operation

Baby Names Australia does not provide user accounts, payment facilities or a private contact form. The website uses Google AdSense to support its operation. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to deliver, personalise and measure advertising where permitted.

If you choose to contact the project through GitHub, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

Hosting and technical information

GitHub Pages

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as IP addresses, browser details and request timestamps. That processing is governed by GitHub's own privacy documentation.

Cookies and future services

Advertising and analytics

Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites. Visitors can manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center. Information about Google's handling of data is available in Google's Privacy Policy.

Where consent is legally required, advertising choices or consent controls will be presented before personalised advertising is used.

External links and children

Important limitations

Links to external websites are governed by those websites' own privacy practices. Baby Names Australia is designed as a general information resource for parents and families and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Questions or correction requests can be submitted through the contact page.
