What this website does

Search, compare and discover

The site helps parents, families and name enthusiasts explore popular baby names in Australia. You can search individual names, compare ranking histories, browse yearly lists and discover classic or emerging choices.

Rankings are presented as a research and comparison tool. A name missing from a published list does not mean that no babies received that name; it may simply fall outside the available ranking range.

How the information is prepared

Public rankings, clearly explained

The project compiles available Australian baby-name ranking information and organises it into consistent profiles and year-by-year lists. Meaning and origin notes are provided as general guidance because interpretations can differ between languages and sources.

For coverage dates, limitations and methodology notes, visit the data sources page. If you find an error, please send the details through the contact page.
