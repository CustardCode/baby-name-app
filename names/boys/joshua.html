The name Joshua

Story and style

Joshua is a biblical and historic name for boys. It is usually linked with Yahweh is salvation, giving it a clear meaning without making the name feel too serious. It is connected to the Hebrew name Yehoshua.

For parents, Joshua is easy to imagine in everyday life: simple enough for a child, but still polished enough to grow with them. It also has a Biblical background, which may appeal to families who like names with Jewish or Christian tradition. Related forms include Josue, Yehoshua. It is often shortened to Josh.
