The name Leon

Story and style

Leon is a historic and traditional name for boys. It is usually linked to the lion, giving it a clear meaning without making the name feel too serious. It has Latin roots and has been used as a given name in European naming traditions.

As a baby name, Leon feels established and familiar without sounding too formal. It can stand on its own or sit beside longer related forms such as Leon, Leonardo, Leopold. Because Leon is already short, it does not really need a nickname, though Lee can work casually.
